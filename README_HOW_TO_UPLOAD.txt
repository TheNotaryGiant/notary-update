HOW TO UPLOAD - 1 STEP, CAN'T MESS UP

1. Go to: github.com/TheNotaryGiant/notary-update
2. Click: Add file > Upload files
3. Drag EVERYTHING from this zip (app folder, public folder, vercel.json)
4. When it asks "Overwrite?" click YES
5. Click green Commit directly to main

That's it. Vercel will auto-deploy in 1 minute.

What this does:
- Adds Blog to top menu (header is navy with gold Blog link)
- Adds /blog page that lists your 3 posts
- Keeps your RSS feed working at /feed.xml
- Keeps all your existing pages

After upload, your menu will be:
Home | Services | Blog (gold) | Book Now
